# Echo-Copilot
This repo is dedicated towards building a next gen copilot that scouts meetings and makes daily to do lists and checklists for members of a team after a scrum or weekly standup call to maintain agile functioning overall without too much hassle and reducing manual checks over time.


# Problem Statement

Agile teams rely on recurring meetings such as daily standups and sprint planning sessions to coordinate work, assign responsibilities, and track progress. However, key outcomes from these meetings—such as action items, task ownership, blockers, and follow-ups—are often recorded manually, inconsistently, or lost across scattered tools.

This project proposes an Agile Meeting Copilot, an AI-powered system that ingests uploaded meeting transcripts and automatically extracts structured Agile artifacts, including sprint-ready tickets, explicitly assigned action items with owners, identified blockers, and key decisions. These outputs are organized within a centralized dashboard that enables teams to maintain accountability and reduce administrative overhead.

The copilot also tracks unresolved tasks and recurring blockers across meetings, providing continuity over the sprint lifecycle and supporting more efficient Agile execution.
